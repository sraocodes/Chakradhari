Why we exist

Decisions about food, water and land are made on incomplete evidence. We build the AI that closes the gap.

Environmental data is sparse and slow. Clouds hide the ground, sensors drift, and satellites miss the moments that matter most. Our systems fill those gaps, show how confident they are, and stay honest when conditions change. We build intelligence you can defend, not dashboards that look convincing until they fail.

The name

Chakradhari means carrying knowledge.

The chakra stands for vision, precision, and purposeful action. We read Chakradhari as carrying knowledge forward. In practice that means turning complex environmental and agricultural data into something clear, and turning clarity into decisions that hold up. It shapes how we work.

  • We carry knowledge.
  • We carry scientific integrity.
  • We carry clarity from complexity.
  • We carry responsible AI.
  • We carry decision intelligence for the real world.
Capabilities

Eight ways we turn measurement into decisions.

We organise around problems, not algorithms. Each of these is a different way to take data from the field and turn it into something a team can act on.

01

Agricultural AI

Models for soil moisture, crop stress, irrigation, and growth. Built for field scale, where ground data is thin and every season is different.

02

Earth Observation AI

We turn satellite radar and optical imagery into calibrated, gap-filled products. Retrieval, fusion, and downscaling, all anchored to local measurements.

03

Scientific AI

Machine learning that respects physics. Hybrid models, parameter estimation, and inverse problems for systems that follow physical laws.

04

Environmental Intelligence

Analytics for watersheds, groundwater, and hydrology. We measure how land and water respond over years, not in single snapshots.

05

Digital Twins

Physics-based simulations, coupled with data. Test scenarios and interventions before you commit them to the ground, with uncertainty included.

06

Agentic AI Systems

AI agents that carry out multi-step scientific work. They retrieve data, run models, and draft analysis, grounded in evidence with a person in the loop.

07

AI Evaluation & Benchmarking

We build the datasets and benchmarks that AI gets measured against. Then we test where models hold, where they break, and whether you can trust them.

08

Data Platforms for Science

Pipelines that turn sensors, satellites, and field records into clean datasets. Quality control, provenance, and licensing come built in, so the work stays reproducible.

How we work

Built to survive contact with reality.

Environmental systems punish models that only look good on paper. Everything we build is designed to stay grounded when the data is thin and the stakes are high.

01

Physics as the backbone

We keep physical laws inside the model. Outputs stay physically possible, not just statistically plausible.

02

Uncertainty as a deliverable

Every estimate carries confidence bounds. Knowing when not to trust a number is as valuable as the number itself.

03

Fusion over single sources

We combine satellites, field sensors, and models. Each covers the others' blind spots, especially when one source goes dark.

04

Validated against the ground

We test models against independent field data, across seasons. Continuously, not once at the end of a project.

Focus areas

Where our experience runs deepest.

The team has years of hands-on research in environmental science and Earth observation. These are the areas where we move fastest and know the ground.

Agriculture & Soil

Soil moisture & crop-water intelligence

We reconstruct soil moisture and crop-water stress by fusing satellite radar with ground sensors. This fills the monsoon and cloud-cover gaps where standard products go quiet.

Water & Watersheds

Watershed & groundwater analytics

We study how watersheds respond over time. That means measuring the effect of interventions, tracking recharge and water availability, and tying both to decisions on the ground.

Earth Observation

Remote sensing & sensor fusion

Retrieval and fusion methods that make satellite data trustworthy at local scale. Everything is calibrated against ground measurements, not taken on faith.

Scientific Software

AI-assisted scientific computing

Hybrid physics and ML models, scientific data engineering, and agentic workflows. We help research and engineering teams run, check, and scale their analysis.

What we're building

The data behind trustworthy AI.

Good AI needs good ground truth. We collect and structure agricultural and environmental data, turn it into benchmarks, and build agents that are measured against them. What we ship can be checked, not just claimed.

01

Curated datasets

We gather field, sensor, and satellite data and turn it into clean, documented datasets. Clear provenance and licensing, ready for science and machine learning.

02

Benchmarks & evaluation

We design evaluation sets that test whether AI is accurate, calibrated, and honest on real agricultural and environmental tasks. Not just on generic leaderboards.

03

Grounded AI agents

We build agents that retrieve data, run models, and draft analysis. They are measured against those benchmarks, and a person stays in the loop.
