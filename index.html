The name

Chakradhari means carrying knowledge into action.

The chakra stands for vision, precision, and purposeful action. We read Chakradhari as carrying knowledge forward. In practice, that means converting deep technical understanding into reliable software, decision systems and infrastructure for teams working on hard scientific problems.

  • We carry knowledge.
  • We carry scientific integrity.
  • We carry clarity from complexity.
  • We carry product thinking into R&D.
  • We carry research into useful systems.
  • We build from India for the wider world.
