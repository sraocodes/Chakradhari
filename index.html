What we solve

Extracting reliable understanding of physical and environmental systems from noisy, incomplete measurements is hard. We build the science and software to do it.

Chakradhari works with research teams, universities and R&D partners on computational problems in sensing, Earth systems and scientific AI — combining electromagnetic sensing, environmental modelling, numerical simulation and physics-aware AI to turn raw observations into scientific understanding. Client and partner work remains confidential unless jointly released.

Company

A deep-tech scientific R&D company, built in Bengaluru for a global research community.

Chakradhari Computational Technologies is incorporated in Bengaluru. We combine electromagnetic sensing, environmental modelling, scientific computing and AI to help research teams turn hard physical-science problems into working systems.

The name carries meaning: the chakra stands for vision, precision and purposeful action — Chakradhari, one who carries it forward. We read that as carrying deep technical knowledge into working systems.
